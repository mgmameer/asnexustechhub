Interview scheduling

We arrange interviews for you with hiring companies. With your consent, your assessment results may be shared with them to support your application.

A friendly note: The Interview Program is separate from the course registration fee. We will walk you through its fee and full written terms during counselling, before you pay anything.
*Program terms and conditions
  • What we guarantee: within 60 days we will arrange (schedule) at least 2 interviews for you with hiring companies. This is a guarantee of interviews; selection and job offers are decided by the employer.
  • When the 60 days start: from the date Nexus confirms in writing that your profile is ready to be shared (resume finalised and eligibility met).
  • Eligibility: you have enrolled in a course, joined the Interview Program, and your education and background suit the roles we will be targeting.
  • Attendance: we ask for at least 75% attendance in live classes, so you are fully prepared.
  • Assignments: please complete and submit your assignments, labs and mock tests on time.
  • Interview participation: please attend each scheduled interview on time and well prepared. If an interview is missed, repeatedly rescheduled or declined, it still counts as arranged.
  • Genuine information: please share true and accurate details in your resume and documents. If details turn out to be incorrect, the guarantee may no longer apply.
  • Reasonable preferences: we ask you to be open to openings relevant to your skills. Very narrow location or salary requirements can limit suitable openings and may extend the time needed.
  • Consent: you allow us to share your profile and, if you agree, your assessment results with recruiters and hiring teams, so we can arrange interviews for you.
  • Hiring companies: we share your profile with recruiters and hiring companies to arrange interviews. These companies may change over time, and we do not guarantee interviews with any particular company, role, location or salary.
  • Outcomes: final selection depends on the employer, your performance and market conditions.
*Refund policy for the Interview Program
  • Interviews arranged: once Nexus has arranged the interviews promised under the program within the 60 days, our commitment under the program is complete and no refund applies. Interviews are arranged by us, but selection is decided by the employer.
  • No interview arranged: if you have met all the conditions above and we have not been able to arrange any interview within the 60 days, you are welcome to request a refund in writing. The program fee is refunded after deducting administration charges and the value of services already delivered (such as resume building), as stated in your written agreement.
  • Conditions not met: if the eligibility, attendance, assignment or interview conditions were not met, the refund does not apply.
  • Course fee: this policy is about the Interview Program fee only. The course registration fee is separate.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

AS Nexus TechHub (“we”, “us”), Hyderabad, Telangana, India, respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect
  • Details you type into our forms: name, mobile number, email (optional), course of interest, skills, experience, city and any message.
  • Whether you are interested in the Interview Program, and, if you accept its terms, the time you accepted them.
  • If you join the Interview Program: your resume, assessment results and other details needed to arrange interviews.
  • A small note stored in your browser to avoid sending the same request twice within a short time, and a simple label for the page you came from. We do not use this website to take card or bank details; UPI payments are made in your own UPI app.
Why we use it
  • To call or message you about demo classes, batches, fees and courses you asked about.
  • To enrol you, run your classes and give career support.
  • For the Interview Program only: to share your profile and, with your consent, your assessment results with recruiters and hiring companies so that interviews can be arranged.
  • To keep records, prevent spam and meet legal requirements.
Who we share it with
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Hiring companies and recruiters, only for the Interview Program and only after you have given consent.
  • Service providers that help us run the website and communicate with you, such as form-handling and spreadsheet tools (Google), WhatsApp, phone and email services.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.
How long we keep it
  • We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete or anonymise it.
Your choices and rights
  • You can ask us to show you the information we hold, correct it, or delete it.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, for example to stop calls and messages or to stop sharing your profile with companies. Withdrawal does not affect what was lawfully done before it.
  • You can raise a complaint with us first, and you may also approach the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • To use any of these rights, write to asnexustechhub@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 7287904544. We aim to reply within one working day.
Children
  • Our courses are meant for adults. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must give consent before you submit your details or enrol.
Security and changes
  • We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or online service can be completely secure.
  • We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
